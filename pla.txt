The interaction between a job seeker and the AI SkillGap Analyzer system is shown in this illustration. The user uploads their resume, which is processed and saved in the database, after logging in and validating their credentials. To conduct a skill gap analysis, the system utilizes the AI/NLP engine to compare user talents against the requirements of the job position. The Job Seeker receives customized suggestions that are created and given to them in light of the results.
